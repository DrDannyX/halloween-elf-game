Elf on the Shelf

Hollow Hill

On Halloween night the Elf crawled off his shelf. He only moves when nobody is watching, and now he's watching you.

Find the 8 cursed candy corns on the hills and upstairs in the old houses, then bring them to the witch's cauldron to banish him. Keep your flashlight on him and don't look away for long.

He isn't alone. Ghosts vanish in your flashlight. Skeletons climb out of their graves, and the light only slows them. Something with eight legs waits upstairs. Every scare drains your courage.

Elf blocking your way? Hold your flashlight on him up close and he'll scurry off to hide. Swing your flashlight to smash skeletons and squash spiders (three hits each).
